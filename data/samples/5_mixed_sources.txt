For my environmental science assignment I chose to write about how plants and climate are connected, because I grew up in a farming village and saw the seasons change over the years.

Present-day climate change includes both global warming, the ongoing increase in global average temperature, and its wider effects on Earth's climate system. The modern-day rise in global temperatures is driven by human activities, especially fossil fuel burning since the Industrial Revolution.

Plants, algae and cyanobacteria use photosynthesis to turn the energy of sunlight into chemical energy that powers their metabolism. This process gives off oxygen as a side product when water molecules are split, and it is the main reason our atmosphere contains oxygen at all.

In our village the monsoon now arrives almost two weeks later than when my grandfather was young, and he says the wells dry up much sooner in summer. I think students like us should plant more trees around the school and track the rainfall ourselves.
